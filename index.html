How Australia Remembers

War cost Australia over 100,000 lives.
Does the way it remembers match that cost?

War memorials stand in communities across Australia. Some honour wars fought close to Malaysia.

Australians served in the Malayan Emergency (1948–1960). They also served in the Indonesian Confrontation, or Konfrontasi (1962–1966).

This page follows four questions. What did war cost, and what shapes does remembering take? Where does it happen, and does it follow the cost?

  • 103,139 Australian war deaths counted by the Australian War Memorial
  • 12,935 memorials in Australia's national register
  • 1,399 memorials in Queensland's register, with map locations

1The cost

The Australian War Memorial (AWM) counts Australia's war dead. Its table covers 36 conflicts and deployments since 1860.

2What Australia builds

Places of Pride is Australia's national register of war memorials. The Australian War Memorial runs it. Anyone can add an entry, so it keeps growing. On 12 October 2026 it held 12,935 memorials.

3Where it is remembered

The national register has no map locations. So this chapter looks at Queensland, which publishes coordinates. Its register covers 1,399 memorials, recorded up to December 2019. They appear in 66 of the state's 78 councils.

761 memorials (54%) honour the First World War.

4Does memory match the cost?

Compare each war's share of deaths and of memorial mentions. If memory followed cost, every line would be flat.

What the data can and cannot tell us

Memorial counts show how often a war is commemorated. They do not measure how important a war was.

The data cannot say why later wars are remembered more.

Remembering is a choice made town by town. It reflects the cost of war, but not only that.

Key terms

Australian War Memorial (AWM)
Australia's national war museum and memorial, in Canberra.
Honour board, honour roll
A list naming people from a community who served.
Cenotaph
A monument to people buried elsewhere.
Malayan Emergency
The 1948–1960 conflict in Malaya, where Australian forces served.
Indonesian Confrontation
The 1962–1966 conflict over Malaysia's formation, called Konfrontasi.
Council
Local government for a town, city or district.
